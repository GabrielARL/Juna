# Milica Stojanovic — OFDM and multicarrier publications

Harvested from <https://millitsa.coe.neu.edu/publications.html> (page generated
2024-09-10), retrieved 2026-08-02. Everything on that page that concerns OFDM,
multicarrier modulation, ICI, Doppler compensation for multicarrier signals, or
capacity of OFDM systems is listed here. Ordering is newest first within each
class, matching the source page.

`[pdf]` marks the twenty-one papers whose full text sits beside this file and
forms the evidence base for the `milica` writing skill. `[sole]` marks papers
where she is the only author — those carry the most weight for style.

## Non-OFDM papers included in the evidence base

Three papers here are not about OFDM. They are included because they carry her
prose at its densest and because two of them supply the physical constants that
every OFDM paper of hers reuses.

| File | Why it is here |
|---|---|
| `2009_stojanovic_preisig_channels_commmag.pdf` | The channel tutorial. Source of the attenuation, noise, multipath and Doppler numbers quoted throughout the OFDM work. |
| `2008_stojanovic_sparse_dfe_phycom.pdf` | Sole-author journal paper. The sparse-channel-estimation argument that the OFDM papers inherit. |
| `2005_stojanovic_diff_coherent_isi_jsac.pdf` | Sole-author journal paper. The differentially coherent detection line that leads to the Aval and Tadayon OFDM work. |

## Reading order, if you read only four

1. `2007_stojanovic_multicarrier_wideband_seatech.pdf` — 1900 words, sole author,
   the whole design argument end to end. Read this first.
2. `2009_stojanovic_mimo_ofdm_asilomar.pdf` — sole author, and the cleanest
   example of her constraint algebra in a conclusion.
3. `2009_stojanovic_preisig_channels_commmag.pdf` — the physics, with numbers.
4. `2015_aval_diff_coherent_multichannel_ofdm_joe.pdf` — a supervised journal
   paper, to see what her style looks like once a student has written the draft.
