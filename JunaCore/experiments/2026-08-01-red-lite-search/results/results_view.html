Reading guide

Definitions, limits, and source coverage for every view.

What does BER count?

ber = bit_errors / attempted_bits over all frames, including failed frames. All 69 rows with a packet success ratio of zero still report BER between 0.12 and 0.31. Neither measure implies the other.

What is effective rate divided by?

effective_rate_bps = successful_frames × payload_bits_per_frame / (frames × frame_duration). Failed-frame time remains in the denominator. It is delivered throughput, not link rate, and it is what the search maximised.

Which configurations are retained?

Only finalists rerun over 60 frames on seeds 6 and 7: four configurations per receiver for each channel and hydrophone, twice, giving 288 rows. The 20-frame screening CSVs remain in the results directory as the search record but are not loaded into this page.

Was every combination tried?

No. The driver descends one dimension at a time and only finalists reached confirmation. Factor levels are therefore unbalanced, and some levels contain only one configuration.

How should the factor ranking be read?

Bar length is the number of decades between the highest and lowest positive median BER for that receiver. An open marker means at least one level reached a median of zero; zero is reported but excluded from the logarithmic span. A long bar identifies a place to inspect, not a cause.

How should the factor panels be read?

Each point is a median BER at one factor level. A hollow point on the floor is an exact zero. A factor's own hold is ignored in its panel so that factor can still vary. Medians are used because BER spans several decades and is skewed.
